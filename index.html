Offvault – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · App: Offvault (com.offvault.app)

In short: Offvault does not collect, upload, sell or share your data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone by itself. Your files only leave the device when you choose Share or Save.

1. Who we are

Offvault is developed by Manish Kumar (“we”). Contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. Data we collect

None. We do not run servers for this app and we do not receive any information from it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

3. What the app processes on your device

To work, Offvault reads and writes data only on your phone:

  • Files you choose – photos, PDFs and documents you pick or share to the app, so it can remove metadata (such as photo location), redact, compress, convert, scan, lock or unlock them.
  • Text recognition – Smart Redact uses on-device text recognition to find ID, card and phone numbers in a photo. The recognised text is used only to place the redaction boxes and is not stored or sent.
  • Encrypted vault – files you add to the vault are encrypted with AES-256 and stored in the app’s private storage. Your PIN is never stored; it is used to unlock the vault’s encryption key.
  • Settings and profile – your choices (theme, auto-lock, number masking) and the optional name you enter in your profile are stored locally in the app.

4. Permissions

  • Biometric / fingerprint – used only if you turn on fingerprint unlock, through Android’s secure biometric system. The app never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • No internet permission – the app is technically unable to connect to the internet.
  • Files are accessed through the Android file and photo pickers, only for the items you select.

5. Third-party components

  • Google ML Kit (text recognition and barcode scanning) – runs entirely on your device inside the app. Its usage-reporting components are disabled in Offvault (the app has no internet permission).
  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner – the “Scan Document” feature opens a scanner that is provided by Google Play services on your phone, which also handles the camera for scanning. Google Play services is a separate Google component and is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy. Offvault receives only the scanned page images that you confirm.

6. Sharing

Offvault never shares your data on its own. When you tap Share or Save to device, Android sends the file you chose to the app or location you pick; that app’s own privacy policy then applies.

7. Backups

  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Offvault, so app data is not copied to Google Drive or another phone automatically.
  • A vault backup file is created only when you choose Export backup. It is encrypted with a password you set, and saved to a location you choose. We cannot open or recover it.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app.
  • Temporary working files are deleted automatically.
  • Uninstalling Offvault deletes all of its data, including the vault. If you lose both your PIN and your recovery key, vault files cannot be recovered – not even by us.

9. Security

Vault files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Screens that show your files block screenshots and screen recording, and an optional app lock asks for your PIN or fingerprint when the app opens.

10. Children

Offvault is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Because the app collects no data, changes will not affect information we hold – we hold none.
